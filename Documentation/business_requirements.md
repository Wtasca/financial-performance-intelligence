## Stakeholders and their requirements
A BI Analyst needs to understand who will use the solution and what decisions they need to make.

Let's define five main stakeholder groups.
Stakeholder	/ Information required	/ Business objective
CFO / Finance Director	/ Overall profitability, revenue, costs and forecasts / 	Strategic financial decisions
Financial Controller	/ Actual vs budget, ledger accuracy, variances / 	Financial control
Finance Manager / 	Departmental expenditure and cost trends / 	Budget management
Product Manager / Product revenue, interest income and margins / 	Product performance
Senior Management / Financial trends, risks and opportunities / Business performance

Stakeholder reporting requirements
CFO / Finance Director
- Monthly financial performance overview.
- Revenue and profit trends.
- Actual versus budget.
- Forecast financial performance.
- Identification of financial risks and opportunities.
Financial Controller
- Departmental expenditure.
- Budget variances.
- Expense categorisation.
- Monthly reconciliation.
- Data quality exceptions.
Product Manager
- Mortgage and savings product performance.
- Net interest income.
- Product margins.
- Average balances.
- Product profitability comparisons.
Finance Manager
- Departmental budgets.
- Spending trends.
- Cost centre performance.
- Overspending alerts.
- Forecast expenditure.
