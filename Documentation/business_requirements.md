## 1. Stakeholder Identification

The project identifies five key stakeholder groups. Each group has distinct information requirements and business objectives that the financial reporting solution must address.

| Stakeholder Group | Information Requirements | Business Objectives |
|---|---|---|
| **CFO / Finance Director** | Overall profitability, revenue, operating costs and financial forecasts. | Support strategic financial planning and decision-making. |
| **Financial Controller** | Actual vs budget performance, general ledger accuracy, financial variances and reconciliations. | Maintain financial control, reporting accuracy and compliance. |
| **Finance Manager** | Departmental expenditure, budget utilisation and cost trends. | Monitor departmental budgets and identify cost management opportunities. |
| **Product Manager** | Product revenue, interest income, product-level costs and profit margins. | Evaluate product performance and identify profitability improvement opportunities. |
| **Senior Management** | Financial performance trends, key risks, business opportunities and strategic indicators. | Assess overall business performance and support organisational decision-making. |

### 1.1 Reporting Requirements

The reporting solution must provide relevant, accurate and timely financial information to support the needs of each stakeholder group.

The solution should enable:
- Strategic monitoring of financial performance and profitability.
- Comparison of actual financial results against budget and forecast.
- Identification of financial variances and cost management opportunities.
- Assessment of product-level financial performance.
- Identification of emerging financial risks and business opportunities.

**Expected Outcome:** A centralised financial reporting solution that translates financial data into actionable business insights, supporting financial control, operational efficiency and strategic decision-making.
